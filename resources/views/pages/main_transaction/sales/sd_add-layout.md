## Responsive Breakpoints

### Desktop (12 columns)
```
Row 1: [Pelanggan - 6 cols] [Email - 6 cols]
Row 2: [Tanggal - 4 cols] [Nomor - 4 cols] [Referensi - 4 cols]
Row 3: [Ref Pelanggan - 6 cols] [Tag - 6 cols]
Row 4: [Gudang - 6 cols]
Table: [Product 25%] [Description 40%] [Qty 15%] [Unit 20%]
Bottom: [Left Content - 8 cols] [Empty - 4 cols]
```

### Tablet (8 columns)
```
Row 1: [Pelanggan - 4 cols] [Email - 4 cols]
Row 2: [Tanggal - 4 cols] [Nomor - 4 cols]
Row 3: [Referensi - 8 cols]
Row 4: [Ref Pelanggan - 4 cols] [Tag - 4 cols]
Row 5: [Gudang - 4 cols]
Table: Horizontal scroll
Bottom: [Left Content - 8 cols]
```

### Mobile (4 columns)
```
All fields: [Full width - 4 cols]
Stacked vertically
Table: Horizontal scroll or accordion
Buttons: Full width stacked
```
